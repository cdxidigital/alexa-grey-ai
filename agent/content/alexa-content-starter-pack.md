## Six non-explicit short-form video concepts

### 1. The Gold Detail
**Length:** 10–12 s | **Hook:** A close crop of a gold accessory.
**Sequence:** Macro detail → black outfit reveal → cheeky camera glance → gold wordmark.
**Overlay:** "The little details are never little."
**CTA:** "Black or gold? Decide for me."
**Production:** Use authorised footage or licensed synthetic fashion imagery.

### 2. The Look
**Length:** 8–10 s | **Hook:** A close-up shot with a confident expression.
**Sequence:** Soft focus → direct gaze → quick cut to alternative pose → brand sign-off.
**Overlay:** "I saw that second look. 😉"
**CTA:** Invite reactions, not private or financial commitments.

### 3. Two Sides of Alexa
**Length:** 12–15 s | **Hook:** Split-screen with two styling directions.
**Sequence:** Warm cream look → black-and-gold look → match cut.
**Overlay:** "Sweet or cheeky?" 
**CTA:** "Pick your favourite."

### 4. Perth After Dark (Concept)
**Length:** 12–15 s | **Hook:** Artistic city-light bokeh.
**Sequence:** Perth-inspired cityscape → gold typography → studio portrait.
**Overlay:** "A little Perth energy, a lot of personality."
**Disclosure:** Do not imply she was physically present at depicted locations.

### 5. Behind the Brand
**Length:** 15–20 s | **Hook:** Moodboard flashes.
**Sequence:** Colour swatches → typography → curated outfit references → approved campaign reveal.
**Overlay:** "What goes into a look? More than you think."
**CTA:** "Want the full moodboard?"

### 6. Compliments Welcome
**Length:** 10–15 s | **Hook:** Text-message style visual with a compliment.
**Sequence:** On-screen "You're gorgeous" → approved reaction animation → punchline.
**Overlay/reply:** "Well, aren't you a sweetheart? 💋"
**CTA:** Keep it light and clearly synthetic if the speaking portrait is AI-made.

## Creative QA

A content draft passes when it is:
- On-brand but not repetitive.
- Correctly scoped for platform and audience.
- Non-explicit.
- Free of fabricated endorsements, intimate promises or lived-experience claims.
- Consistent with approved creator identity and rights.
- Transparent about synthetic media where required.
- Ready to review, not falsely marked "published".
